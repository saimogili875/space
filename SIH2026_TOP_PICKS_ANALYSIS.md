**Scoring Criteria:**
- **Real Impact (R):** Does it solve a genuine, measurable problem? Saves money/time/lives?

- **Niche Factor (N):** Fewer teams likely to pick it = less competition?

## THE SWEET SPOT FORMULA

**Best PS = Niche Domain + Clear Metric + Available Data + Small Scope + Visible Demo**

- Niche: Mining + geology (few teams touch this)
- Clear metric: "Predict reserves with X% accuracy, flag shortfall Y days early"
- Data: Sentinel-2 satellite imagery, geological surveys, production history
- Scope: Focused on one company (MOIL) and one mineral (Manganese)
- Demo: Satellite heatmap showing reserve probability + production forecast dashboard
